How I work

Four things, in the order they usually have to happen.

01 / Positioning & packaging

Turn a capability into an offer

Positioning and messaging frameworks, buyer personas, segment-level value propositions, service model and pricing structure, launch plans. The output is something a salesperson can carry into a room and a buyer can approve.

02 / Partner & ecosystem growth

Sell through other people

Partner and channel development, co-selling and referral motions, ecosystem-led growth across the Middle East and Sub-Saharan Africa, and market entry into the GCC from outside it. An unknown brand borrows trust faster than it builds it.

03 / Enterprise account growth

Grow the book you already have

Owning a portfolio of enterprise accounts through upsell, cross-sell, retention and executive reporting. 73+ accounts at Citibank, 15+ at The 808, 10+ key accounts at The Teaser Company.

04 / Enablement & operations

Make it repeatable

Sales decks, brochures, case studies, proposal and quote templates in CRM, playbooks, pipeline reporting and forecasting. The test is whether the motion still runs when I am not in the room.
